# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Everyday UK growers, including beginners, who want clear guidance for a garden, allotment, balcony, or small growing space.

## Product Purpose

GrowGuide UK turns a grower’s location, conditions, space, and crop choices into useful actions. It helps people decide what to do now without making them learn gardening jargon or manage an account.

## Positioning

GrowGuide UK combines postcode- and weather-aware guidance with a small, private user profile saved on the user’s device. The service favours relevant actions over a large library of generic gardening content.

## Operating Context

People use GrowGuide UK while planning or tending their growing space. They may return throughout the season to review local weather, current priorities, crop timing, and personalised advice.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- A short setup records postcode, growing conditions, plot size, equipment, and chosen crops.
- The browser stores the profile and saved advice locally.
- The service uses external postcode and weather services to provide local guidance.
- AI advice is a paid operating cost and must remain bounded.
- The product requires no user account.
- User-facing text, interruptions, and pop-ups must remain minimal.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: GrowGuide UK.
- Domain: growguideuk.co.uk.
- Voice: practical, calm, concise, and encouraging.
- The identity must look distinct from growguide.co.uk and avoid a generic sprout or seedling mark.
- The site should feel more sophisticated without becoming formal, ornate, or difficult to use.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing crop data, seasonal tasks, postcode lookup, weather display, and advice workflow in the application.
- Existing garden photography in `public/images`.
- No testimonials, user counts, or performance claims are available and none should be invented.

## Product Principles

- Give people the next useful action.
- Make local conditions matter.
- Collect only the data needed to help.
- Keep the interface quiet and direct.
- Spend paid AI calls deliberately.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keep the service usable on phones and desktops with keyboard access, readable contrast, clear labels, and plain language.
