Autumn on the plot isn't just about pulling up what's finished. It's the season that sets you up for spring.

I've put together three free guides for GrowGuide UK to help: what to clear now and what to leave for wildlife, how to cover your soil for winter (mulch, green manure or leafmould), and a proper end-of-season clean for your tools and greenhouse.

All three are simple two-page PDFs you can save or print and keep in the shed.

Take a look and download your free copies here:
https://growguideuk.co.uk/resources
